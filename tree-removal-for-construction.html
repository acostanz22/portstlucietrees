Construction site clearing

Tree Removal for Construction in Port St. Lucie, FL

Builds, additions, and driveway expansions need trees out of the footprint and the access path. Construction tree removal clears that wood on a schedule your contractor can work around.

Request an estimate Call 772-492-4436

Clearing for a build

Construction removal is different from a single hazard tree. You may need several trees down, brush cleared, and stumps gone below grade. Inside Port St. Lucie, protected trees on the parcel can still need permits even when the lot is vacant.

When to call

  • A survey shows trees in the building pad or drive.
  • Trucks cannot reach the site until a tree is gone.
  • An addition needs clearance off an existing house.
  • The builder asked for stumps out, not just trunks cut.

How the work goes

  1. Review the stakes, survey, and trees marked to keep.
  2. Remove trees in the cut list and protect keepers.
  3. Grind or remove stumps as specified.
  4. Haul debris so the site is open for the next trade.

What changes the price

  • Number and size of trees.
  • Stump specification from the builder.
  • Access for trucks on a raw lot.
  • Permit needs for protected trees.

Benefits of scheduling tree work before dirt work

Equipment shows up to a clear pad instead of waiting on saws. You also avoid discovering a protected trunk after the crew is already mobilized.

Common construction clearing problems

Trees marked save versus remove, stumps left too high for grading, and debris piles in the truck path. We price the scope the builder actually needs.

Pricing

Per-tree and per-stump counts matter. Lot clearing rates apply when brush and saplings fill the parcel beyond a few specimen trees.

Permits and keepers

Flag protected trees early. Cutting the wrong oak can stop a project. We work from your marked list.

Questions about this job

Can you work from a builder's tree survey?

Yes. Send the plan and the mark legend. We cut what is listed.

Do you grind stumps for slab work?

Yes when specified. Depth matters for graders and footers.

Is this the same as land clearing?

Related. Construction removal often pairs with lot clearing when brush fills the same pad. We can price both.

How far ahead should we book?

As soon as stakes are in. Storm season and other removals fill the calendar.

Talk through the tree

Call or send the property details. We will tell you if the job is a removal, a trim, or something that should wait for the utility.
