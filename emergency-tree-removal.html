Where emergency tree removal fits local properties

If a tree is on the house or blocking the only way out, call and describe what you can see. Do not stand under a hanging limb, and do not touch a tree that is in a power line.

When to call

  • A tree or large limb is on the roof, the carport, or a parked car.
  • The driveway or the front walk is blocked.
  • A trunk is split and still standing over the house.
  • Wind left a palm or oak hung up in another tree.

How the work goes

  1. You call and tell us what fell, what it hit, and whether wires are involved.
  2. If a power line is in the tree, you call the utility and we wait until the line is safe.
  3. The crew secures the load, cuts the weight off the structure, and moves the wood.
  4. We clear a path and leave the remaining cleanup listed so you know what is left.

What changes the price

  • What the tree hit and how much of it is still attached.
  • Whether the work is on a roof or only in the yard.
  • Access after other debris is already down.
  • How much wood has to leave the property the same day.

What changes the job on Treasure Coast lots

Sandy soil, tight setbacks, pool cages, and summer wind all change how a tree comes down. A photo of the trunk, the house, and the access path is enough to start an honest estimate.

What an estimate covers

The estimate should include the tree size, how close it is to structures, equipment needs, haul-away, and stump grinding if you want it. We do not publish a flat price list.

Questions about this job

Should I call you or my insurance company first?

If someone is hurt or a line is down, call emergency services and the utility first. For a tree on the house with no wires, call us to make it safe and call your insurer to ask what they will cover. We do not decide the claim.

Can you tarp the roof?

We remove the tree. Roof tarping and structural repair are a roofing job. Once the trunk is off, a roofer should cover the opening so rain does not follow the tree inside.

Do you work during a storm?

We work after the wind drops enough to cut safely. Climbing or running a bucket in active severe weather is how people get hurt. Call when it is safe to be outside and we will take the jobs in the order we can reach them.

What if the tree belongs to my neighbor?

The tree that falls on your house is still your immediate problem. Who pays can depend on whether the tree was already dead and on your policies. We can remove it from your side. The cost question is between you, your neighbor, and the insurers.

Talk through the tree

Call or send the property details. We will tell you if the job is a removal, a trim, or something that should wait for the utility.
