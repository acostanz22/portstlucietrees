Trees near structures

Tree Removal Near Houses & Buildings in Port St. Lucie, FL

A tree against a roof, garage, or commercial wall has no room to fall. Removal near houses and buildings means dismantling the tree so wood never free-falls onto the structure.

Request an estimate Call 772-492-4436

Working next to structures

Port St. Lucie setbacks put many shade trees within limb-reach of tile roofs and lanais. The safe method is top-down removal with ropes or a crane when needed. Open-yard felling is the wrong tool when a building is in the lean.

When to call

  • Limbs are on or over the roof already.
  • The trunk is within a few feet of a wall or cage.
  • A tree sits between two buildings with no drop zone.
  • You need the tree gone before a roof or addition project.

How the work goes

  1. Protect windows, cages, and landscaping where practical.
  2. Remove the canopy over the structure first.
  3. Lower trunk sections away from the building.
  4. Clean the work zone so contractors can start if that is next.

What changes the price

  • How close the trunk is to the building.
  • Roof pitch and cage complexity.
  • Need for a crane versus climbing gear.
  • Haul-away and stump work in a tight yard.

Benefits of professional near-structure removal

You avoid the common mistake of notching a tree and hoping it falls away from the house. Controlled removal protects roofs, screens, and fences.

Common problems

Limbs growing into gutters, trunks planted too close decades ago, and trees that settled toward the house after storms. DIY felling in these spots is how walls get hit.

Pricing

Tight access and protection time are the cost drivers. A tree five feet from a wall costs more than the same tree in an open backyard.

Coordination with other trades

If a roofer or builder is next, tell us the schedule. We can clear the tree and leave access open.

Questions about this job

Can you work over a pool cage?

Yes, with care. Panels and screens are easy to damage, so wood is lowered, not dropped.

What if the tree is touching the roof?

We remove contact wood first so the roof is clear, then finish the trunk.

Do you tarp damaged roofs?

We remove the tree. Roof sealing is a roofer's job after the wood is off.

Can the stump stay?

Yes if you want. Grinding in a tight side yard may need a second visit with a smaller machine.

Talk through the tree

Call or send the property details. We will tell you if the job is a removal, a trim, or something that should wait for the utility.
