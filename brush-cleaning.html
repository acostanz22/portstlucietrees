Where brush cleaning fits local properties

Brush against the house holds moisture, hides pests, and gives a ground fire something to run through. Cleaning it is a haul-away job, not a weekend of bags at the curb.

When to call

  • Palm debris and leaves have matted along the foundation.
  • Underbrush has filled a side yard you cannot walk.
  • A storm left a layer of small limbs the city will not take.
  • You want the beds cleared before a fence or a paint job.

How the work goes

  1. Walk the area and confirm what stays, including plants you planted on purpose.
  2. Cut and rake the debris out of the beds and off the walls.
  3. Load it. Brush from a Florida yard does not fit in household trash.
  4. Leave the beds open so you can see the soil and the foundation.

What changes the price

  • How deep the pile is and how far it runs.
  • Whether vines have to be cut out of a fence or off a wall.
  • Access for a trailer.
  • Haul-away volume.

What changes the job on Treasure Coast lots

Sandy soil, tight setbacks, pool cages, and summer wind all change how a tree comes down. A photo of the trunk, the house, and the access path is enough to start an honest estimate.

What an estimate covers

The estimate should include the tree size, how close it is to structures, equipment needs, haul-away, and stump grinding if you want it. We do not publish a flat price list.

Questions about this job

Is this the same as lot clearing?

No. Brush cleaning is the piled debris and undergrowth around an existing yard. Lot clearing is a larger cut on an overgrown parcel. If you are not sure which you need, send a photo.

Will you pull vines off the house?

We can cut vines at the base and pull what comes free without tearing siding or screens. Vines grown into a wall may need a painter or a carpenter after the green growth is gone.

Do you take palm trunks in this service?

Small debris, yes. A standing palm or a large trunk is a removal, priced on its own. We would rather split the jobs in the estimate than surprise you.

Can the debris stay as mulch?

Chips can stay if you want them. Most homeowners want the pile gone because it is full of seed and palm stalks, not clean mulch.

Talk through the tree

Call or send the property details. We will tell you if the job is a removal, a trim, or something that should wait for the utility.
